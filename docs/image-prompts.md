## Attach reference images — this matters more than the words

ChatGPT cannot remember the photographs it made for this brand. Written style notes get you
close, but attaching real examples gets you much closer. **Before pasting a prompt, attach two
or three of the existing images to the conversation** and say:

```text
Here are photographs from an existing set for this brand. Match their lighting, colour grade,
uniforms and overall feel exactly in the image I am about to describe.
```

**Deep Cleaning · Service hero + service card** · priority P1 · aspect 3:2 · 2400 × 1600 px

The cleaner is correctly standing on the floor with a telescopic pole — only the ladder has to go.

```text
Photorealistic editorial photograph for a commercial cleaning brand called Clario Cleaning, set in Belgium. Shot on a full-frame camera. Natural daylight only — no flash, no harsh shadows, no heavy vignette. Warm, calm, premium magazine feel. Colour grade: warm ivory highlights, muted forest and sage greens, subtle sand and oak accents, low saturation. Setting: a modern Belgian commercial interior — office, shop or hospitality space. Clean but believably lived-in, never sterile or staged. Any cleaning staff wear plain forest-green (#244F41 family) polos or work jackets with at most a small ivory CLARIO wordmark on the chest, and charcoal trousers. They look competent and unhurried, working rather than posing at the camera. Faces are often turned, in profile, or softly out of focus. Equipment is realistic: microfibre cloths, flat mops, refillable spray bottles, tidy trolleys.

WHY THIS IMAGE IS BEING REDONE:  the supplied version has a step ladder in frame. The Deep Cleaning page states plainly that work at height is outside the concept's scope ("no ladders, lifts or rope access"), so the image contradicts the copy. The cleaner is correctly standing on the floor with a telescopic pole — only the ladder has to go. Please make sure the new version does not repeat it.

SUBJECT: Two cleaners carrying out a periodic deep clean in a restaurant dining room after hours: chairs on tables, one cleaning skirting/table bases.

COMPOSITION: Wide; subjects within central 50%.

CAMERA: Eye level or slightly low, 28–35mm.

LIGHTING: Warm evening interior light mixed with dusk window light.

BACKGROUND: Contemporary Belgian bistro, wood tables, warm pendant lights.

PEOPLE: Yes — 2 cleaners.

UNIFORM: Forest-green (#244F41 family) short-sleeve polo or zip work jacket, charcoal work trousers, small ivory CLARIO wordmark on chest (text only, no other logos), clean non-slip shoes. No gloves unless handling products.

ASPECT RATIO: 3:2, exported at 2400 × 1600 px.

MUST NOT INCLUDE:
- No text, letters, numbers, captions, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image. (The first batch came back with "Before Cleaning" burned in — the website adds its own labels in three languages, so any text in the image has to be cropped out.)
- No readable brand names or third-party logos on products, packaging, signage or screens.
- Uniforms stay exactly as established: plain forest green, at most a small ivory "CLARIO" wordmark on the chest.
- Never show work at height — no ladders, scaffolding, lifts or rope access. Everything reachable from the floor.
- No medical or hospital cues: no hazmat suits, no face masks, no clinical rooms.
```

**Save as** `service-deep-hero.png` (or .jpg/.webp) in `src/assets/clario/`.

## If you ever regenerate the before/after pair

CL-06 and CL-07 must be produced **as one scene in two states**: identical room, identical
camera position, identical lens, identical light — only the mess changes. The reliable way is
to generate the clean "after" frame first, then ask ChatGPT to *edit that same image* to add
believable end-of-day mess (cups, crumbs, smudges, chairs pushed out), rather than generating
two images separately. Any shift in angle is immediately visible when the slider moves, and
would misrepresent a cleaning result.
